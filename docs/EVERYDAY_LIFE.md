# Everyday life

The **Character → Everyday life** ledger displays wellbeing, accounts, credit, home, career, skills, business and estate beneficiary. **Act** contains the new activities, under the existing Body and mind, School and Money groups. Home shows happiness and stress. No network or AI API is required.

This is an original implementation of life-simulation categories observed in the supplied Verdant Life APK, adapted to Port Harlan. No APK source, text, artwork or assets are included. It does not reproduce every Verdant subsystem: detailed specialist careers, trading exchanges, countries, social media, sports and production chains are outside this change.

## Rules

- Time still moves only through Age up. Monthly accounting runs only for months actually elapsed, including interrupted age-ups. Actions still share the existing six energy per turn.
- Happiness and stress range from 0 to 100. Work and crime raise stress; rest and family focus reduce it. At 85 stress, health loses five points each month after ordinary recovery. Low happiness and high stress reduce career performance. Take time for yourself spends one energy to reduce stress by 25 and increase happiness by 10.
- Creative, technical and enterprise skills range from 0 to 10. Practice takes one energy. Creative or technical skill unlocks paid freelance work; enterprise level 2 unlocks a service business.
- Savings earn 2.5% yearly, applied monthly with rounding. Investments have seeded monthly returns from -6% to +7%, with no guaranteed return. These simplified game rates are not intended to model real markets. Transfers spend no energy and transfer $1,000 in 2026 purchasing power, adjusted to the current era; the actual price is shown on each action.
- One personal loan per turn: $5,000 in 2026 purchasing power, credit 580 minimum, no outstanding loan. Interest is 12% annually, charged monthly. Payments are 2% of the current balance, at least $25 in 2026 money. Payments are taken only when cash covers them; missed payments reduce credit. Manual repayments charge the exact remaining balance, even in older eras.
- Homes cost 180 times the district's base monthly rent. Purchase requires age 18, credit 620, annual job pay at least one fifth of price, and a 20% down payment. The remaining 80% is a 30-year mortgage at 5%. Annual upkeep is 1.5% of current home value. Ownership replaces rent only in the home's district. Moving elsewhere adds rent; mortgage and upkeep continue, including during prison. Values vary -3% to +6% on birthdays. Deep cash arrears force a sale at 90% of value, settle the mortgage and reduce credit. Voluntary sales deduct 5% selling costs.
- A career tracks months in the current job and performance. Promotion requires 12 months, 70 performance and one energy; it raises pay 15%. At most one promotion per turn, up to level 5. Switching jobs resets progression. Retirement from age 65 pays 35% of final salary annually; work focus will not automatically find another job. Deliberately finding a new job ends retirement when monthly accounting resumes.
- A service business costs $3,000 to open. Each month it generates cash based on level, enterprise skill and reputation, with a 15% chance of an operating loss. Expansion costs $1,000 times current level and one energy, up to level 5. Businesses pause in prison. Closing stops future results and gives no resale payment.
- A will can name one living child. After death, choose any living child to continue. Positive net worth is liquidated at ledger values, with mortgages and personal debt deducted, and allocated to the named living heir or divided among living children. Unpaid debt is not inherited. A different child can be played but receives no share if a living beneficiary was named. The new generation keeps the date and life log, with relatives reassigned. Skills, career, accounts and powers are generated/reset for the heir rather than copied from the deceased. NPCs store only birth year and a Graded flag, so exact NPC birthday, gender and power identity cannot be reconstructed.

## Saves and validation

Existing version-1 saves gain default everyday-life fields during migration, without changing cash or consuming dice. New fields persist through autosave, slots and export codes. Engine tests cover migration, deterministic accounting, era-adjusted transfers, exact repayments, gating, mortgages, promotion, retirement and estate settlement. Build and the complete existing suite must pass before publishing changes.
