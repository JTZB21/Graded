FORM EP-6 · LIFE CYCLE

Aging changes everything

Powers follow the body's arc. In this world, getting older changes your power, your career, your family, and how people see you.

The Kindling

85% of powers appear between 12 and 21, often triggered by fear, injury, or grief. You have 72 hours to report to the Bureau for Assessment.

The Fade

From about 45, body and energy powers weaken; mind and perception powers often grow subtler. Corporate heroes are quietly retired after 40.

Going dark

About 40% lose their powers after 60. About 3% suffer Inversion, where the power turns against the body. Children of a Graded parent: 1 in 40 Kindle.

FORM EP-7 · STANDING

Where you stand

Rank decides your job, your rent, your dating pool, how police treat you, and your sentence. Graded rank each other ruthlessly, and baselines resent all of them.

StrategicsS · SS · Ω

National assets. Rich, guarded, tracked by transponder, and unable to travel freely. Feared even by other Graded.

HighgradesA · B

Celebrities and contractors. Exclusive clubs, sponsorships, practical immunity from minor laws. "Bottom-B" is an insult among them.

Mid / LowgradesC · D

The working class of the powered world. Utility jobs, cops, street heroes, gang muscle. Many live in the Old Foundry.

DudsE · F

All of the registration, none of the perks. Mocked by Graded and baselines alike. Some find that a "useless" power is priceless in the right job.

Baselines99.98% of people

Worship, envy, fear, and hate the Graded, often all at once. Graded call them "blanks"; they call Graded "freaks" and "juicers."

FORM EP-8 · LAW

Licenses and the law

Registration is mandatory, and staying unregistered is a felony. Using powers in public requires a license, and sentences grow with your rank.

Civilian

The default. No public power use. Courts rarely accept self-defense from a Graded.

Utility

Power use for one job: construction, firefighting, medicine, security.

Vigilance

Hero work. Needs B-Rank (or C-III+), insurance, and a sponsor who owns your image.

Strategic

S-Rank and above. Military service, an approved contract, or a guarded compound.

The Bureau

The Federal Bureau of Enhanced Affairs (1962). Registers, assesses, licenses, and hunts. Its armed wing is Containment.

Quell

A power suppressant (since 2003). Shuts powers off for 4–72 hours. Painful, addictive, and weak against A-Rank and above.

The Sump

A supermax 400 m under Nevada for B-Rank and above. Quell every 72 hours, no daylight, known only by rank and number.

FORM EP-12 · PROCEDURE

How the game plays

The Narrator runs the world and every NPC, and never speaks or decides for you. Be a hero, a villain, or neither. Real dice decide uncertain outcomes, and every NPC gets a fresh, uncommon name.
